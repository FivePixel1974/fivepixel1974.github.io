Banana Frenzy

Catch bananas, build streaks, chase high scores, and survive the frenzy!

A fast-paced jungle arcade adventure from Five Pixel Games. Keep your catcher moving, time your catches, and make every basket count as the action ramps up.

Explore gameplay Screenshots Privacy policy

Catch the good. Avoid the rotten.

Use left and right movement to line up your basket with incoming bananas. Build a streak with successful catches, watch for special bananas, and stay alert when the pace picks up.

01 / Catch

Every basket counts

Position your catcher carefully and land bananas in the basket to keep your run alive.

02 / Score

Build your streak

Chain successful catches, push your score higher, and chase a new personal best.

03 / Power-ups

Special banana surprises

Look out for Golden Bananas, Shield Bananas, and Slow-Time Bananas. Watch out for Rotten Bananas.

04 / Challenge

50 levels and Endless Mode

Progress through 50 levels, then see how long you can keep the frenzy going in Endless Mode.
