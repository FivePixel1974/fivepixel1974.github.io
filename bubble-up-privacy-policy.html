Bubble Up

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Five Pixel Games handles information in connection with Bubble Up.

Bubble Up does not require a player account, and Five Pixel Games does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information through the game’s core gameplay.

1. Information stored on your device

Bubble Up stores game-related information locally on your device when storage is available. This may include sound preferences, best score, best stage, and local leaderboard records with optional three-letter player initials. This information supports game features and is not sent to a Five Pixel Games player server.

2. Personal information

Bubble Up does not require an account or your full name, email address, phone number, account credentials, precise location, contacts, or photos to play. Entering three-letter initials for the local leaderboard is optional, and those initials remain stored on your device.

3. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current development version of Bubble Up does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated tracking services to collect player information. If advertising or other third-party services are added to a future version, this policy and any applicable store disclosures will be reviewed and updated before or alongside that release.

4. Third-party platforms

If you obtain Bubble Up through a third-party platform such as Google Play, that platform may process information independently under its own terms and privacy policy. Five Pixel Games does not control data collected directly by the platform outside the app.

5. Data sharing and sale

Five Pixel Games does not sell personal information from Bubble Up. Because the game’s current local settings and leaderboard information are intended to remain on your device, Five Pixel Games does not share that local game information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local settings, scores, stages, and leaderboard initials may remain on your device until you clear the relevant browser or app storage or uninstall the game, depending on the platform. Five Pixel Games does not operate a player account or remote leaderboard database for this local game data, so there is no corresponding server-side player account to delete.

7. Security

Bubble Up is designed to store its current settings, scores, and local leaderboard information on the player’s device rather than on a Five Pixel Games server. Device and platform security controls apply to information stored locally.

8. Children’s privacy

Bubble Up does not require players, including younger players, to submit personal information to Five Pixel Games in order to play. Optional local leaderboard initials do not require an account. If the app’s intended audience, features, or data practices change, Five Pixel Games will review and update this policy and applicable platform disclosures.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bubble Up’s features, distribution methods, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.
