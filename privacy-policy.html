Banana Frenzy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Five Pixel Games handles information in connection with Banana Frenzy.

Banana Frenzy does not require a player account, and Five Pixel Games does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information through the game’s core gameplay.

1. Information stored on your device

Banana Frenzy may store game-related information locally on your device, such as settings and local leaderboard information. This information is used to provide game features and is not intended to be transmitted to Five Pixel Games.

2. Personal information

Banana Frenzy does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal details in order to play.

3. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of Banana Frenzy is intended to operate without Five Pixel Games using advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated tracking services to collect player information. If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or alongside that change.

4. Third-party platforms

If you obtain Banana Frenzy through a third-party platform such as Google Play, that platform may process information independently under its own terms and privacy policy. Five Pixel Games does not control data collected directly by the platform outside the app.

5. Data sharing and sale

Five Pixel Games does not sell personal information from Banana Frenzy. Because the game’s current local settings and leaderboard information are intended to remain on your device, Five Pixel Games does not share that local game information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data may remain on your device until it is cleared through the applicable browser, app, or device controls, or until the app is uninstalled, depending on the platform. Five Pixel Games does not operate a player account or remote database containing this local game data, so there is no corresponding server-side player account to delete.

7. Security

Five Pixel Games designs Banana Frenzy so that its current settings and local leaderboard information can remain on the player’s device rather than being sent to a Five Pixel Games server. Platform and device security controls apply to information stored locally.

8. Children’s privacy

Banana Frenzy does not require players, including children, to submit personal information to Five Pixel Games in order to play. If the app’s audience, features, or data practices change, Five Pixel Games will review and update this policy and any applicable platform disclosures.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Banana Frenzy’s features, distribution methods, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.
