Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Five Pixel Games handles information through our website and our games, including Banana Frenzy and Bubble Up. It applies to the editions and platforms on which those games are made available. Features and third-party services may differ between versions.

Our games do not require a player account. Game settings, progress and local leaderboard information are designed to stay on your device. When an advertising-enabled edition uses Google AdMob, the advertising SDK may separately collect and share certain device and usage data as explained below.

1. Game information stored locally

Banana Frenzy may store settings, gameplay-related preferences and local leaderboard information on your device. Bubble Up may store sound preferences, best score, best stage and local leaderboard records, including optional three-letter initials. This data supports gameplay features and is not intended to be uploaded to a Five Pixel Games player account or remote leaderboard server.

Neither game requires you to register an account or submit your full name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos in order to play. Any optional leaderboard initials are intended to be stored locally.

2. Advertising in our games

We are preparing free, advertising-supported mobile releases of Banana Frenzy and Bubble Up. Development builds and versions without ads may not contain an advertising SDK. In an edition where ads are enabled, we plan to use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display and measure advertising. The SDK may automatically collect and share the following information with Google:

  • Approximate location: an IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location, without requesting precise GPS location for advertising.
  • App interactions: information such as app launches, taps, ad interactions and video views.
  • Diagnostics: technical performance information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
  • Device or other identifiers: information such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account-related identifiers.

Google describes the purposes of this processing as advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, which may include security and compliance. This is separate from our locally stored game settings, scores and leaderboard initials. Data processing can occur as part of ordinary ad delivery, even where ads are non-personalised.

More information is available in Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. The data actually collected depends on the SDK version, features and configuration in the particular game release.

3. Advertising choices and consent

We intend to use age-appropriate advertising controls, including non-personalised advertising where required. Non-personalised advertising does not mean that no technical data is collected. Where applicable law or Google policy requires consent for advertising-related processing, we will implement the necessary consent and privacy-choice mechanisms before enabling affected advertising. Players may also have advertising ID and privacy controls in their device settings or through Google's My Ad Center.

Consent screens or in-game privacy controls described here apply only if provided in the relevant release. We will review the actual advertising setup and update this policy and each app's store disclosures if our use of advertising services changes.

4. Website information and contacting us

Our website provides information about Five Pixel Games and links to our games and public pages. You do not need to create an account to browse it. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser requests as part of providing and securing the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide. We use these messages to respond to enquiries, support requests and privacy questions. Links to third-party websites, such as Facebook or app stores, are subject to those services' own privacy practices.

5. Third parties and data sharing

Where enabled, Google AdMob may collect and share the advertising information described in section 2. Google Play and other distribution platforms may also process information independently under their own terms. Our website hosting provider processes information needed to serve the website. We do not sell locally stored player settings, scores or leaderboard records to data brokers.

6. Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local game settings, progress, scores and leaderboard initials may remain on your device until you clear the relevant app or browser storage or uninstall the game, depending on the platform and its backup settings. Five Pixel Games does not currently provide player accounts or a hosted database of these local gameplay records, so there is no corresponding Five Pixel Games player account to delete.

Messages sent to us by email are kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, maintain necessary records or meet legal obligations. You can contact us to ask about personal information you have provided directly to Five Pixel Games or to make a privacy request, subject to applicable law.

Advertising information handled by Google is subject to Google's own retention, deletion and privacy controls. Clearing local game data does not necessarily delete information already processed by Google or an app distribution platform. For relevant controls, refer to Google's privacy resources linked above or contact us for assistance understanding the options.

7. Security

We design local gameplay records to stay on the player's device where practicable. According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK documentation, data transmitted by that SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Device, hosting-provider and app-store security measures also apply to their respective services. No method of electronic processing is completely risk-free.

8. Younger players

Our games are planned for teenage and adult audiences and are not specifically directed to children younger than 13. We do not require children to provide personal contact information to play. Age-related protections differ across countries, and some players under 18 may be considered children under applicable rules.

Before enabling advertising, we will review age-appropriate ad SDK configurations, consent requirements and any required age-screening or restrictions, including Google Play Families requirements where applicable. Ads delivered to children or users of unknown age must meet applicable rules. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Five Pixel Games, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games, website, advertising partners or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy at this same address and update the effective date when material changes are made. Each game's store-specific Data Safety declaration will describe that particular released app.
